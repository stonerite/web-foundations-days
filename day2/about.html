About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for jotting down short thoughts, tasks and reminders the moment they come to mind. It keeps everything on one page so you can capture an idea in seconds and get back to what you were doing.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your idea into the "New note" box.
  3. Press the "Add Note" button to save it.
  4. Check the "Your notes" list to see everything you have captured.

Features

  • Quick, distraction-free note entry
  • A running count of your notes
  • A clean, easy-to-read list of everything you have saved
  • Simple navigation between pages

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you are typing
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field or button
